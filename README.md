# Nova Mart Retail Ltd. — 2024 Sales & Profitability Dashboard
End-to-end Power BI retail analytics project exploring sales trends, regional performance, and customer purchasing insights for Nova Mart Retail Limited.


An interactive two-page Power BI business intelligence report analyzing 2024 retail sales performance, regional profitability, product category revenue, customer segments, and discounting impact for *Nova Mart Retail Limited*.

## Executive KPIs (2024)

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Total Sales* | *$1.2M* |
| *Total Profit* | *$47.6K* |
| *Profit Margin* | *3.82%* |

---

## Dashboard Overview

### Page 1: Executive Summary & Core Performance
- *KPI Summary Cards:* High-level tracking of *Total Sales ($1.2M), **Total Profit ($47.6K), and **Profit Margin (3.82%)*.
- *Monthly Sales Performance:* Line chart tracking revenue trajectory across the 12 months of 2024, highlighting stronger Q1–Q2 performance followed by lower second-half volume.
- *Regional Performance (Sales vs. Profit):* Side-by-side horizontal bar comparisons across *East, **Central, **West, and **South, revealing that while **Central* and *West* generate strong top-line sales, *South* outperforms both in net profit behind *East*.
- *Product Category Performance by Sales:* Column comparison across *Technology, **Furniture, and **Office Supplies*, showing balanced revenue contribution led by Technology.
- *Top Product & Customer Performance by Sales:* Ranked breakdowns identifying top revenue-generating sub-categories (led by *Phones, **Chairs, and **Accessories) and top-performing customers (Samuel Ade, **Fatima Bello, and **John Doe*).

### Page 2: Segment, Sub-Category & Discount Analysis
- *Sales Breakdown by Customer Segment:* Pie/donut breakdown across *Consumer, **Corporate, and **Home Office* segments.
- *Sub-Category Performance Comparison:* Detailed horizontal ranking comparing sales/profit contribution across product lines.
- *Profit & Discount Performance:* Multi-column evaluation examining how discounting affects profit margins across product tiers.
- *Monthly Sales Distribution:* Column view reinforcing seasonal revenue patterns across the year.

---

## Key Analytical Insights

1. *Top-Line vs. Bottom-Line Divergence by Region:* *East* leads in both sales and profit, whereas *Central* ranks second in revenue but drops behind *South* in profitability—pointing to higher discounting or cost pressure in the Central and West regions.
2. *Balanced Category Revenue:* Revenue is evenly distributed across *Technology, **Furniture, and **Office Supplies, reducing over-reliance on a single product line, with **Phones* and *Chairs* driving sub-category volume.
3. *Margin Compression Opportunity:* An overall *3.82% profit margin* on *$1.2M* in sales indicates significant room to improve net profitability by reviewing discount structures in lower-margin regions and sub-categories.

---

## Strategic Recommendations & Solutions

1. *Audit & Cap Discounting in Central and West Regions:* Implement tiered discount approval thresholds in *Central* and *West* to bring their profit conversion closer to *East* and *South*, protecting bottom-line margin without sacrificing high order volume.
2. *Replicate High-Margin Product Bundling:* Pair high-volume drivers (*Phones, **Chairs, and **Accessories) with higher-margin **Office Supplies* and service add-ons to lift the overall company profit margin above *3.82%*.
3. *Target Second-Half Revenue Campaigns:* Launch targeted Q3–Q4 promotional campaigns for *Corporate* and *Home Office* segments to counter the post-Q2 revenue slowdown and smooth out seasonal dips.
4. *Strengthen Key Account Retention:* Establish loyalty incentives and dedicated account check-ins for top revenue contributors (*Samuel Ade, **Fatima Bello, and **John Doe*) to protect repeat business.
